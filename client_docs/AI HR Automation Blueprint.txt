Contents1. Purpose 
To automate talent decisions using structured  

job data, employee data, and AI-driven  
scoring. 

2. Scope 
Applies to: 
External hiring 
Internal mobility 
Succession planning 
Promotions 

3. Inputs 

Job Description (from JD Engine) 
Candidate CVs / employee profiles 
Performance data 
Competency framework 
Business rules (eligibility, tenure)

4. Process Steps 

A. Hiring SOP 

• HR creates vacancy request 

• System retrieves approved JD 

• AI generates job advertisement 

• CVs are uploaded or integrated 

• System extracts CV data (skills, experience) 

• AI scores candidates vs JD 

• System ranks candidates 

• AI generates interview questions 

• Interview feedback is collected VIDEOS 
• AI generates hiring recommendation 

• HR confirms or overrides decision

AI RECRUITER ENGINES4. Process Steps 

B. Internal Mobility SOP 

• Internal role is published 

• System scans employee profiles 

• Skills and competencies are matched 

• AI scores role fit 

• Skill gaps are identified 

• Recommendation report generated 

C. Succession SOP 

• HR defines critical roles 

• System retrieves performance & competency 

data 

• AI identifies successors 

• Readiness level is assigned 

4. Process Steps 

D. Promotion SOP 
• Promotion cycle initiated 
• System validates eligibility rules 
• Performance history reviewed 
• AI generates promotion score 
• Recommendation submitted for approval 

5. Outputs 

Ranked candidate lists 

Mobility recommendations 

Succession matrices 

Promotion justification reports
