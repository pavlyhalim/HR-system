AI HR STRATEGY ENGINE – BACKEND ARCHITECTURE MAP 

Note 
This is a high-level backend system map. 
AI HR Strategy Engine is a governing intelligence layer, not a reporting tool. 
Final scope, timelines, and cost depend on phase prioritization. 

1. CORE SERVICES 

A. API Gateway 

• Request routing 
• Authentication & Authorization 
• Rate limiting 
• Request logging 
• CORS handling 
• Payload validation 
• Tenant & strategy isolation 
• Strategy execution audit middleware 

B. Authentication Service 

• User login / logout 
• JWT token generation 
• Role-based access control (HR, Executive, Manager, Strategy Owner, Auditor) 
• Session management 
• Strategy approval permissions 
• Read vs Execute access enforcement 

2. BUSINESS LOGIC SERVICES 

A. Strategy Definition Service (Strategy-as-Data) 

Endpoints 
POST /api/strategy 
GET /api/strategy 
GET /api/strategy/{id} 

 
 
 
 
PUT /api/strategy/{id} 
POST /api/strategy/{id}/version 

Business Logic 
• Store HR strategy as structured objects, not documents 
• Strategy composed of: 

•  Strategic pillars 

•  Strategic initiatives 

•  KPIs 

•  Roadmap phases 

• Full strategy versioning 
• Editable without regeneration 
• Immutable historical versions 

B. Strategic Pillars Service 

Endpoints 
POST /api/strategy/pillars 
PUT /api/strategy/pillars/{id} 
DELETE /api/strategy/pillars/{id} 
GET /api/strategy/pillars 

Business Logic 
• Pillars defined dynamically (not hard-coded) 
• Pillar attributes: 

•  Name 

•  Description 

•  Priority 

• Pillars reorderable 
• Pillars linked to business objectives 
• Reusable across strategy versions 

C. Strategic Initiatives Service (Executable Strategy Layer) 

 
 
Endpoints 
POST /api/strategy/initiatives 
PUT /api/strategy/initiatives/{id} 
GET /api/strategy/initiatives 
PATCH /api/strategy/initiatives/{id}/status 

Business Logic 
• Each initiative linked to: 

•  One strategic pillar 

•  One or more KPIs 

• Initiative attributes: 

•  Objective 

•  Scope 

•  Owner 

•  Timeline 

• Status tracking (Planned / Active / Completed) 
• Initiative dependency management 

D. Strategy–Execution Linkage Service 

Endpoints 
POST /api/strategy/initiatives/{id}/link-module 
GET /api/strategy/initiatives/{id}/execution-status 

Business Logic 
• Link initiatives to execution engines: 

•  Recruitment & Mobility 

•  Performance Management 

•  TNA 

•  Workforce Planning 

• Configurable linkage (per initiative) 
• Execution feedback loop into strategy 
• Broken / inactive links flagged 

 
E. Strategy Readiness Scoring Service 

Endpoints 
POST /api/strategy/readiness/calculate 
GET /api/strategy/initiatives/{id}/readiness 

Business Logic 
• Readiness score per initiative (0–100) 
• Factors: 

•  Organizational maturity (OMA) 

•  Workforce capability 

•  Cultural readiness 

•  Resource availability 

• Scoring logic configurable 
• Stored with initiative version 

F. Strategy Confidence Engine 

Endpoints 
GET /api/strategy/confidence 

Business Logic 
• Aggregate readiness scores 
• Assign confidence level: 

•  High 

•  Medium 

•  Low 

• Recalculated on any data change 
• Executive-facing confidence signal 

G. Strategy Benchmarking Service 

 
 
 
Endpoints 
POST /api/strategy/benchmark 
GET /api/strategy/benchmark/report 

Business Logic 
• Benchmark strategy against: 

• 

Industry standards 

•  Company size 

•  Region 

• Pillar-level comparison 
• Variance detection (Above / At / Below) 
• Benchmark dataset versioning 

H. Strategy Explainability & Risk Service 

Endpoints 
GET /api/strategy/explainability 
GET /api/strategy/initiatives/{id}/risk 

Business Logic 
• Explain: 

•  Why pillars were selected 

•  What data influenced initiatives 

• Business-language explanations 
• AI reasoning traceability 
• Risk of non-execution analysis: 

•  Operational 

•  Talent 

•  Financial 

• Severity scoring
