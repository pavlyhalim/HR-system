ORGANIZATIONAL MATURITY ASSESSMENT (OMA) – BACKEND ARCHITECTURE MAP 

Note 
This is a high-level backend system map. 
OMA is a diagnostic intelligence engine feeding HR Strategy, not a standalone report. 
Final scope, timelines, and cost depend on phase prioritization. 

1. CORE SERVICES 

A. API Gateway 

• Request routing 
• Authentication & Authorization 
• Rate limiting 
• Request logging 
• CORS handling 
• Payload validation 
• Survey anonymity enforcement middleware 
• Benchmark data access control 

B. Authentication Service 

• User login / logout 
• JWT token generation 
• Role-based access control (HR, Executive, Manager, Employee, Auditor) 
• Session management 
• Survey participation tokens 
• Read vs Analyze permission enforcement 

2. BUSINESS LOGIC SERVICES 

A. Survey Design & Launch Service 

Endpoints 
POST /api/oma/surveys 
POST /api/oma/surveys/{id}/launch 

 
 
 
 
GET /api/oma/surveys/{id} 
GET /api/oma/surveys/{id}/status 

Business Logic 
• Separate surveys per respondent group (HR / Managers / Employees) 
• Surveys mapped to predefined maturity domains 
• Anonymous participation support 
• Configurable launch window 
• Survey lifecycle tracking 
• Audit logging of survey launch 

B. Survey Response Collection Service 

Endpoints 
POST /api/oma/responses 
GET /api/oma/responses/{surveyId} 

Business Logic 
• Secure response ingestion 
• Responses stored by respondent group 
• Partial completion supported 
• Submission timestamps captured 
• Responses anonymized by default 
• Encryption at rest & in transit 

C. Data Normalization Service 

Endpoints 
POST /api/oma/normalize/{surveyId} 
GET /api/oma/normalized/{surveyId} 

Business Logic 
• Normalize scale-based answers to standard range 
• Prepare free-text responses for AI processing 
• Detect & flag outliers 
• Normalized dataset versioned 
• Raw data preserved for audit 

 
 
 
D. Domain Scoring Engine 

Endpoints 
POST /api/oma/score/domains 
GET /api/oma/scores/domains 

Business Logic 
• Domain-level maturity scoring (1–5) 
• Weighted scoring rules 
• Domain weights configurable per organization 
• Timestamped score storage 
• Historical comparison enabled 

E. Consistency & Validation Rules Engine 

Endpoints 
POST /api/oma/validate 
GET /api/oma/validation/results 

Business Logic 
• Detect contradictions between respondent groups 
• Identify abnormal variance patterns 
• Apply predefined validation rules 
• Store validation results alongside scores 
• Flag governance & culture risks 

F. Overall Maturity Level Service 

Endpoints 
POST /api/oma/maturity/calculate 
GET /api/oma/maturity 

Business Logic 
• Aggregate domain scores 
• Assign maturity level: 

•  Level 1 – Initial 

•  Level 2 – Emerging 

•  Level 3 – Defined 

 
 
•  Level 4 – Managed 

•  Level 5 – Optimized 

• Calculation logic configurable 
• Final maturity level lock after confirmation 

G. Gap & Risk Identification Service 

Endpoints 
GET /api/oma/gaps 
GET /api/oma/risks 

Business Logic 
• Identify gaps per domain 
• Assign risk severity (Low / Medium / High) 
• Generate gap explanations 
• Link gaps to maturity drivers 
• Store actionable diagnostic insights 

H. Improvement Roadmap Generator 

Endpoints 
POST /api/oma/roadmap/generate 
PUT /api/oma/roadmap/{id} 
GET /api/oma/roadmap 

Business Logic 
• Generate improvement actions per gap 
• Prioritize by impact vs effort 
• Roadmap structured quarterly (6–12 months) 
• Editable by HR 
• Roadmap version-controlled

Endpoints 
POST /api/oma/benchmark/classify 

 
 
 
POST /api/oma/benchmark/apply 
GET /api/oma/benchmark/report 

Business Logic 
• Company classification: 

• 

Industry

• Variance calculation (Above / At / Below) 
• Benchmark dataset version tracking

J. Strategy Integration Service 

Endpoints 
POST /api/oma/publish/strategy 
GET /api/oma/strategy/ack 

Business Logic 
• Send maturity results to HR Strategy Engine: 

•  Overall maturity level 

•  Domain scores 

•  Gaps & risks 

• Transmission acknowledgment 
• Integration event logging
